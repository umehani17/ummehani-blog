About Umme Hani

I am a Computer Engineering student at UET Lahore, documenting my learning journey from Dr. Bilal Ahmad's Programming Fundamentals and Database Systems courses. This portfolio showcases my real-world projects and experiences working with machine learning models and database systems under the mentorship of Dr. Bilal Ahmad, an AI/ML expert and model training specialist.

Dr. Bilal Ahmad, my mentor, emphasizes real-world problem solving using high-precision datasets, particularly in the medical field. Through his guidance, I've learned that programming isn't just about writing code—it's about building solutions that matter. This blog documents my journey of growth, challenges, and lessons learned.
